What the comparison reveals

Public institutional capability does not always translate into CIFAL programming.

Strong host AI, limited centre evidence

AIT, FIEP/SENAI, the University of Surrey, ECUPL, UVic and York illustrate ways to reach existing research, policy or technical expertise beyond what each centre’s slide deck documented. These are collaboration leads, not proof that expertise is currently being shared.

AI activity has multiple meanings

AI certificates, AI-policy research, operational chatbot pilots and industrial research labs are distinct. The matrix avoids treating any single category as equivalent to technical deployment or institution-wide maturity.

Practical conditions for any pilot

Apply these before moving a host capability into CIFAL delivery.

Access and readiness

Confirm the named technical owner, staff time, permission to collaborate, facility or tool access, data rights, availability of experienced trainers, participant demand and a measurable learning outcome.

AI safeguards

Check data protection, Indigenous data sovereignty where relevant, accessibility, bias and language performance, human decision authority, safety-critical use restrictions and a transparent evaluation plan.

04 / METHODOLOGY & SOURCES

What has been verified and what remains unknown.

The host directory is treated as the governing source for institutional identities. External evidence is attached to the named host, not borrowed from unrelated partners. Centre activities are read from the previously prepared 2026 slide-deck-only reviews.

Reading rules

Use the atlas as an evidence inventory and a collaboration prospecting resource, not a conclusive capacity audit.

Important limitations. Public web pages do not prove that CIFAL has access to a host’s AI expertise, staff or systems. The lack of a public AI record is not proof of absence. The depth and freshness of institutional public reporting vary. Recent external press claims are flagged as needing first-party validation. The user’s Shanghai host note remains open for legal confirmation.
RULE 01

Hosting ≠ partner

For Miami, OAA is host; FIU is an institutional partner. For Japan, Iwaki City is host. The Málaga host/operating association distinction is preserved.

RULE 02

Training ≠ deployment

Academic curricula and AI discussions establish capacity-building evidence, but not a live AI application unless one is separately documented.

RULE 03

Proposal ≠ active work

Each recommended collaboration is prospective and requires a named contact, data agreement and practical pilot validation.

How evidence statuses were used

Documented 2 in the export

An AI activity, team, course, software system or collaboration is explicitly described by a relevant institutional or corroborating source.

Qualified 1 in the export

A related digital capacity exists, a project is planned, or an external report requires confirmation. Do not count it as deployed institutional AI.

Not verified 0 in the export

The reviewed sources do not establish the capability. This does not constitute an absence finding or a rating of institutional quality.
